Protect Your Property from Termites

Termite treatment can help protect buildings and wooden structures from termite activity. A To Z Cleaner offers pre-construction and post-construction termite treatment, subject to site assessment and service confirmation.

Available Services

  • Pre-construction termite treatment
  • Post-construction termite treatment
  • Inspection of reported termite concerns
  • Treatment planning based on property conditions
  • Guidance on preparation before treatment

How It Works

  1. Choose Termite Control on the booking page.
  2. Provide the property size and location.
  3. We discuss the property condition and treatment requirements.
  4. The treatment method, schedule and price are confirmed before work.

Before Treatment

Treatment requirements vary according to the building, the extent of termite activity and the selected method. Follow the service provider's instructions regarding people, pets, food and access to treated areas.

Important:

Treatment results and protection periods depend on the treatment method, site conditions and product used. No fixed protection period is guaranteed on this page.
