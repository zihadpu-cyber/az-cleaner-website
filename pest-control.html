Professional Pest Control

Protect your home and workplace from common pests with a treatment plan suited to the property and pest problem.

How Our Pest Control Service Works

  1. Initial assessment: We discuss the pest problem and affected areas.
  2. Treatment planning: We select an appropriate method based on the pest and property.
  3. Targeted treatment: Treatment is carried out in the agreed areas using suitable products and procedures.
  4. Safety guidance: We explain any precautions, ventilation requirements and re-entry instructions applicable to the treatment.
  5. After-service advice: We provide practical steps to help reduce future pest problems.

What This Service Can Address

  • Cockroaches
  • Ants and other crawling insects
  • Mosquitoes and selected flying insects
  • Other pests, subject to assessment

Before the Service

Please tell us which pests you have noticed and where they appear. Follow the specific preparation and safety instructions provided before treatment.
