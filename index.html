    Our Policies

    1. Booking Policy

    Customers can book cleaning services through our website or WhatsApp. Bookings are subject to availability and confirmation.

    2. Prepaid Discount

    Customers who choose prepaid payment will receive a 10% discount on the applicable service price.

    3. Cancellation & Refund Policy

    Customers who cancel their booking at least 1 day before the scheduled service will receive a full refund of their prepaid amount.

    For cancellations made less than 1 day before the scheduled service, please contact our support team for assistance.

    4. Pricing Policy

    Service prices and estimates are based on the selected service and applicable details. The final price will be confirmed before the service.

    5. Service Policy

    Customers should provide accurate booking details and ensure that our cleaning team can safely access the service location.

    6. Payment Policy

    Customers must pay the confirmed service amount using the available payment methods. Any applicable discount will be communicated before payment.

    Need help? Please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.
